Privacy notice

Effective 22 August 2026

Current prototype

Candidate inputs are processed in the browser for this demonstration. The current prototype does not create an account, send profile data to a third party, or run advertising analytics.

Data retained

Profile fields, strategy rows, audit decisions and the locked snapshot stay in the active browser session. Refreshing or closing the page clears the working record.

Cookies and tracking

CounselFlow does not set tracking cookies in this prototype. Font files may be requested from Google Fonts when the page loads.

Candidate control

Do not enter names, phone numbers, application credentials or payment details. Use only the counselling facts required to prepare a preference strategy.
