Planning aid

CounselFlow prepares and audits a preference strategy. It is not an official UPTAC, AKTU or institute portal and it cannot submit or alter a counselling choice on the candidate's behalf.

Verify evidence

Fees, closing ranks, distance and placement evidence may be incomplete or change by counselling round. Confirm every final choice against current official notices.

Candidate decision

Recommendations and conflict warnings explain supplied facts. The candidate remains responsible for the final order and for every choice submitted to the official portal.
